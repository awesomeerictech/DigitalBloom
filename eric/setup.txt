An app for uploading menu items and their respective categories



Digitalmenu is an App intended to be used by restaurants,hotels,eateries,clubs,bars,motels and other places where foodstuffs and drinks are sold or served, these users i mean hotels,bars etc are supposed to register a new account and verify their phone numbers using a code that will be messaged to them, after account registration they have the full rights to upload a menu item and their respective category and view them, once a user registers a new account they have the rights to even delete and update menu items belonging to them, after being contented that they have done the right thing these users can download the Qr image that was generated by the app automatically , print it and place it at a strategic position in their business places, customers can scan the Qr code using any Qr scanning app in the google play store and open the URL containing the menu items for that particular business, customers can even filter menu items based on their name and categories, that's all for Digitalmenu .
